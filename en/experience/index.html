Two formats

At a cupping table

Private coffee cupping

You step into the role of a barista. We explore coffee plants, examine samples and trace the story of the process. You taste several coffees and regions and learn to name fragrance, aroma, body, acidity, sweetness, flavor, balance and aftertaste.

  • How to choose method, ratio and grind size to brew better at home.
  • Why the grinder matters more than it seems: conical versus flat burrs.
  • Concrete reasons to choose good beans.
On foot, with three stops

Specialty coffee tour

A walking tour designed for visitors and locals. We go as regular customers, without booking ahead with the coffee shops, so we can judge their service and coffee without an advantage.

  • Coffee history, plant samples, processes and bean defects.
  • How to appreciate coffee through different methods and evaluate them.
  • How to choose a quality bean.

Dates, price and meeting point

Write to us for dates and price.

Languages: Spanish, English, Portuguese and French. We confirm the language the day before, if all participants agree.
