Frequently asked questions

The essentials about the experience, booking and the book. If something is missing, message us on WhatsApp.

What is the Beantage experience?

It is two formats for learning specialty coffee in Bogotá, Colombia: a private cupping and a walking tour through three coffee shops. Both are designed so you understand what is in the cup and learn to choose good beans.

How do I book?

Message us on WhatsApp at +57 313 398 0338 with the format you want and how many people are coming. You can also book on Viator or Tripadvisor from the experience page.

Is there a group discount?

Yes. Groups of 4 to 8 people get 20% off when they book through our platform or on WhatsApp.

What languages is it offered in?

Spanish, English, Portuguese and French. We confirm the language the day before, if all participants agree.

What is the coffee tour like?

It is a walking tour with three stops. We go as regular customers, without booking ahead with the coffee shops, to judge their service and coffee without an advantage.

What is the gift book?

Whoever buys the experience receives "Specialty Coffee: From Seed to Cup", by Luis Felipe Ariza Vesga. Chapter 1 can be read for free on the book page.

What is specialty coffee?

According to the book, it is coffee that scores 80 points or more on a scale of 0 to 100, measured in a cupping: a systematic way to taste coffee so that two people in different rooms can agree about the same cup.

How much does it cost and where do we meet?

We have not published a price or meeting point on this page yet. Message us on WhatsApp and we will send them with the available dates.
