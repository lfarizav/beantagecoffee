The book

Specialty Coffee: From Seed to Cup

History, plant, processing, roasting and cupping of specialty coffee. Author: Luis Felipe Ariza Vesga.

A book by Luis Felipe Ariza Vesga, roaster and guide at Beantage Specialty Coffee Roasters in Bogotá. Fifteen chapters that follow coffee from the legend of Kaldi to the cup you pour at home.
